Product

Courtship, as a managed workflow.

Three systems that remove the guesswork from a process that had exactly one step.

Pebble Escrow

Courtship pebbles are held by a neutral third-party skua until both parties commit to the nest. If he ghosts, you keep the pebbles. Gravel is detected at intake and returned to sender with a formal note.

Vocal ID

Upload one contact call. We fingerprint the waveform, notarize it, and surface you to males within earshot. Replaces the legacy discovery system, which was shouting.

Molt Mode

Your profile auto-hides for the duration of your catastrophic molt. You return to the queue in three weeks, fully waterproof, with your match rate and escrow balance intact.
