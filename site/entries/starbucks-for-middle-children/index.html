§ 01

What the store does

Birth-Order Queueing

Orders are sequenced against documented birth order, imported from your family group chat. Firstborns are made first because they always were. The youngest is made next because the youngest is still the youngest. Yours comes out at a sensible point after that, warm enough.

Approximate Name Capture

The cup printer renders a name within two characters of the one you gave. Internal research found that correcting a barista was the longest conversation 61% of our members had that week, so the opportunity is preserved at every store.

Table Topology

Every Mezz floor plan is certified middle-seat-only. No corner two-tops, no window banquette, no stool at the end of the bar. You are seated between a first date and a man on speakerphone, which is where you were going to sit anyway.
