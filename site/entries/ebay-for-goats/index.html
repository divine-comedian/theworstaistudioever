CONDITIONS OF SALE

How the barn runs

Read aloud by the auctioneer before every sale. Nobody has listened yet.

LOT 0001

Chew Before You Buy

Every lot comes with a 30-second sample window so bidders can assess quality by mouth. Most lots do not survive the sample window. The seller is notified and paid in sympathy.

LOT 0002

Headbutt to Bid

Bids are placed by striking the device firmly with the forehead. One headbutt raises the bid by one mouthful. Our hardware partners have stopped returning our calls, which we read as confidence.

LOT 0003

Fence-Post Seller Ratings

Sellers are scored out of five posts for honesty, shipping speed and whether the item arrived already partly eaten. The marketplace average is 2.1 posts and has been 2.1 posts since launch.
