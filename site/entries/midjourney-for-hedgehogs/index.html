/describe capabilities · confident, wrong, shipping

Built for how you actually forage.

Three capabilities, developed over four winters with a focus group of one hedgehog who has since left the program.

input · multimodal

Snout-native prompting

A pressure-sensitive input pad translates snuffling into structured prompts. Independent benchmarks put intent accuracy at 11%, which our benchmarks describe as state of the art. The other 89% of renders are damp leaves, which our users describe as fine.

compute · circadian

Dusk-to-dawn rendering

Jobs queue at sunset and finish by sunrise, aligned to your circadian rhythm. Prompts submitted in daylight are discarded without notification, out of respect. Our GPUs also prefer it cooler.

safety · non-negotiable

Predator-negative by default

Every model ships with --no owls, --no badgers, --no traffic baked into the weights. This cannot be toggled in settings. Removing it requires a written waiver, a witness, and a second, braver hedgehog.

/show community · verified insectivores only

The colony is rendering.

Reviews from the public gallery. Portraits render in queue order. Two of these members submitted their headshot request during daylight.
