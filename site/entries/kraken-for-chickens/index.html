Three properties we have stopped apologising for.

Our members are birds. Every safeguard below follows from that, and each one was reviewed by counsel before it was priced.

Custody

Cold storage, in the literal sense

Member keys are printed at four point on a paper slip and placed beneath a broody hen. Retrieval requires the hen to stand up of her own accord. Clutch does not hurry the hen. Median withdrawal time is nine days and we consider that a security property.

Consensus

Proof of Lay

The ledger advances only when a member lays. Average block time is twenty-six hours and falls to nothing in November. Finality is therefore seasonal, which our counsel has asked us to describe as finality anyway.

Access

Roost-order authentication

Every dusk the flock sorts itself onto the perch in a stable hierarchy. That sequence is your second factor. Introducing a new bird rotates the credential for the entire pen, a behaviour we have documented and priced rather than fixed.

Members on the record.

Quotations collected at the north run, transcribed by a human, approved by the pen.
