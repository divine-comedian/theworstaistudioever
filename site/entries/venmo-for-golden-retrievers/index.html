How the money moves

Three features, each of which passed review by a committee of dogs who were also, at the time of review, eating the committee's notes.

Visible to everyone, including the cat

A public feed of everything

Every payment posts to a feed the whole dog park can read. Our research team asked 2,400 golden retrievers about privacy settings. All 2,400 wagged. We removed the settings.

Split by the centimetre

Split the stick

Two dogs, one stick. Ballpark splits ownership to the centimetre and sends a payment request to whichever dog is currently chewing the other end.

Median stick: 61 cm½ each
Under 4 seconds, squirrels aside

Instant settlement, mostly

Balls arrive in under four seconds unless the recipient sees a squirrel, in which case the transfer is pending indefinitely. Ballpark does not insure against squirrels. Nobody does.

Pricing, in dollars, which your dog does not have

Billing goes to the human whose card is on file. The dog chooses the tier. This has caused some friction.
