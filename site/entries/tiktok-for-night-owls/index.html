The mechanics

Scarcity, disclosure, and a well-formatted email.

Three systems, each running exactly as designed. We are proud of two of them.

Availability

The Gate

smallhours does not open before 11pm local time. Daytime launches show a countdown and nothing else. Demand isn't created here; it's fermented.

Ranking

Drowsy Ranking

Every video is scored by its measured ability to keep a tired person awake. High scorers get boosted after 2am, when you're most receptive. This is disclosed here, once.

Accountability

The Sunrise Report

Each morning at 7am we email you a complete account of the night. "You viewed 61 videos about canal locks between 3:04 and 4:17." Accountability is a feature. Unsubscribing is not.
