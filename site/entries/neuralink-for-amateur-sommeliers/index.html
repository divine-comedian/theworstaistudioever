ART. 1 · SPEECH

Verbal Override

The implant intercepts your sentence 40 milliseconds before speech and replaces “it's fruity?” with the certified descriptor. You hear yourself say it at the same time everyone else does. Most users describe this as a relief.

ART. 2 · VISION

Optic Abstention

Blind tasting, enforced at the hardware level. Palais suspends your optic nerve at the table so the label cannot bias you. Vision resumes once you have committed to an opinion on record.

ART. 3 · CALIBRATION

Reference Palate Sync

Nightly firmware aligns your taste receptors to a Master Sommelier in Lyon. Within six weeks you will be indistinguishable from him. Known side effects include hating everything you can afford.

Back Label · Surgical Terms

PALAIS® is an investigational Class III device and an estate-bottled point of view. It is not approved by the FDA, which has asked us to stop describing the review process as “still in the decanter.” Induction is performed by a board-certified neurosurgeon or a person the concierge describes as equivalent. Removal is available at any time and is billed as a second induction.

Conviction is a measure of delivery, not accuracy. Palais does not improve your palate; it improves your account of it. In the event of a firmware disagreement between you and Lyon, Lyon prevails. Do not operate heavy machinery while describing Beaujolais. If you begin to detect notes of forest floor in foods that are not wine, keep a log; we collect those.
