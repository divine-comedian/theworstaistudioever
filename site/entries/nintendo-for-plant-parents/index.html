Manual p.07 / System behaviour

How the console handles a plant

Three decisions the hardware makes on your behalf, documented here so that support calls can be shorter.

Bay 01
01

One plant, one cartridge

Every plant in your home ships as a physical cartridge, moulded to fit exactly one bay. Adding a fern means buying a fern. Cartridges are region-locked to your hardiness zone and will not boot at a friend's house, which we consider correct behaviour.

2ABS 09-26
Bay 02
02

Permadeath ships enabled

When a plant dies, the save is erased at the cartridge level rather than the console. Competing systems let you roll back a drought. We evaluated that feature for two years, built it twice, and removed it both times.

2ABS 09-26
Bay 03
03

Difficulty is assigned, not chosen

The console reads the light in the room and sets the difficulty itself. Fiddle-leaf fig has never been rated below EXPERT in any household, in any season. The rating is written to the cartridge and cannot be lowered by you, by us, or by an authorised service centre.
