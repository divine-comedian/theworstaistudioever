What Taciten does

  • Three mechanisms
  • Always on
  • Not adjustable in flight
1.1

Sentence ceiling

Set a hard word limit per utterance. On contact with the ceiling, Taciten closes the sentence for you using the nearest available grammatical exit. In trials, 71.4% of closures landed on “so yeah.”

1.2

Anecdote deduplication

The implant indexes every story you tell and tags it against the listener who heard it. The second time you begin the one about the airport, your jaw is not consulted.

1.3

Floor yield

Taciten monitors the room for a person who has been waiting to speak for more than 4.0 seconds and administers a brief, non-negotiable pause. The pause is not adjustable. The pause is the product.

Warning: loss of anecdote

Deduplication is permanent. Taciten does not archive the stories it withholds. A story suppressed four times is generally not recoverable, and users report retaining the setting of the story without the events in it.

Taciten cannot distinguish between a story your household has heard once and a story your household has heard eleven times. It treats both as heard. Discuss this with the people you live with before the ceiling is set below 40 words.

Do not operate Taciten during a eulogy, a toast, a deposition, or any setting in which the length of your contribution is the point. Suppression events during these settings are recorded but not refunded.
