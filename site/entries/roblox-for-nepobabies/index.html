The platform, as it was explained to us by the client's father.

Three systems, each maintained by people you will not meet.

Pre-completed obbies

Each obstacle course is finished by a licensed professional before you log in. You spawn on the final platform beside the trophy, and the badge is already in your inventory. Jumping is optional and, for liability reasons, discouraged.

The Robux Trust

Your in-game currency sits in an irrevocable trust administered by your father's business manager. Withdrawals need two signatures, neither of them yours. Interest compounds quarterly and is reported to your mother's accountant in Zurich.

Merit Mode

One toggle rewrites the credits on any game you own. You become Founder, Lead Designer and Visionary. The 41 contractors who built it move to a scrolling "Special Thanks" panel set in 9-point type.
