How this inventory demo works
  1. You own two locomotives. Model A is already in your personal reserve, leaving one available to buy.
  2. Add Logo size as a product option and assign Small to Model A, Large to Model B.
  3. Select Keep one of each variant. Physical stock stays at two, personal reserve becomes two, and available stock becomes zero.
  4. Publish product to publish that state. Uncheck the reserve setting and publish again to make the large-logo model available.

The cart is a preview and never takes payment. Reset demo restores the original two models and clears publication.
