Preview Walks

Every behavioral change gets an isolated preview walk before it merges to the main route. Stakeholders (the neighbors, one squirrel) can comment directly on the walk. Nothing reaches your living room until it has been approved by at least one reviewer who is also a dog.

License No. K9-2026-002

Instant Rollover

One click reverts your dog to the last deployment that did not involve the trash. Mid-zoomies, mid-bark, mid-shoe: state restores to asleep-on-the-cold-tile with zero downtime and no memory of the incident, on either side.

The Edge (of the Yard)

Your dog is replicated across 119 kennels of presence worldwide, so there is always a dog within 40 milliseconds of your hand. Fetch requests route to the nearest available dog. Cold starts apply to senior dogs only, and we think that's fair.
