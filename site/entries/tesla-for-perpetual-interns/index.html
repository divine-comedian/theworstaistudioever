Three things the pack does without asking the cells.

Trickle sits between your headcount plan and the people in it. It reads, it redistributes, and it forecasts. It does not explain.

Cell balancing

One intern doing everything is a pack under stress. Trickle detects the imbalance, routes that cell's work across the four cells nearest zero, and reports the rack as nominal by 09:00. The overperforming cell is not notified, because notification is a discharge event.

Regenerative recognition

Praise is energy and energy should not be lost to the room. Trickle captures the kinetic component of a compliment at the moment it is delivered, stores it against the cell's ledger, and returns 4.10% of it to the cell on a ninety-day delay. The remaining 95.90% is retained at the pack level.

Degradation forecasting

Trickle projects remaining cycles from commute distance, lid-open hours, unprompted Sunday messages, and the frequency of the phrase happy to help. Renewal paperwork is generated on the last cycle the model rates as good. Accuracy holds to 91.30% across four consecutive internships.
