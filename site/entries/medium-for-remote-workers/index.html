The apparatus

The Editorial Desk

Every update is assigned to a working editor before your team is allowed to see it. Median time from filing to publication is two business days, which internal research confirms is roughly when the blocker resolves itself. The piece runs anyway, because the desk does not kill copy for being late to its own subject.

¶Editors are assigned, not requested. Requesting an editor by name is grounds for reassignment to the weekend desk.

Members-Only Blockers

Put the part where you say what is wrong behind the paywall. Teammates get three free reads per calendar month. After that they subscribe, or they escalate, or they guess. All three outcomes are counted as engagement and reported to the desk on Fridays.

¶Escalation is a conversion event. Your on-call rotation is a funnel and has been since March.

Read Time as a Unit of Work

Timesheets are denominated in estimated minutes to read. A four-minute Thursday is a four-minute Thursday and reconciles cleanly against the sprint. Finance stopped asking follow-up questions in the second week, which we log as adoption and cite in the renewal deck.

¶Word count is not billable. Only the reading of it is billable.
