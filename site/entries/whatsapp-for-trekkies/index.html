What the service does to your messages

glazing schedule / lights I–III

Three behaviours, all of them mandatory, none of them configurable. They apply on the free tier and on the eight thousand four hundred dollar tier in exactly the same way.

Light I

Transit is calculated, not simulated

You enter your position. We take it at face value. Every message is then held for the exact interval a signal would need to cross that distance, down to the second, and released without further comment. Most crews sit between four and eleven minutes apart. One pair sits at 4.24 years. They have filed nothing.

Light II

Every channel has a bridge

Group chats are issued a roster: Comms, Helm, Science, Engineering, Medical. Only the officer holding Comms may transmit. Everyone else files into the suggestion log, which Comms is under no obligation to open. Rotation runs weekly, is published fourteen days out, and cannot be edited by anyone including us.

Light III

Continuity is reviewed before delivery

An on-device reviewer checks each outbound message against 1,140 hours of established material. A contradiction is not deleted. It is held, logged, cited, and attached to your continuity score, which is visible to every member of the channel and does not reset.
