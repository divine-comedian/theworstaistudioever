What the network does

Three systems, none of them optional.

Range does not run experiments on the ranking model. The ranking model is the pack, and the pack was here first.

Howl Relay

Posts propagate by acoustic handoff. One howl moves your photograph roughly 11 km, then holds until a neighboring pack repeats it. Median delivery is four nights. Weather adds two. We have modeled this extensively and consider it acceptable.

Hierarchy Feed

The feed is ranked by rank. Breeding pairs surface first in every territory they border, permanently. Mid-pack wolves may purchase placement by the night. Omegas are shown their own posts, which our retention data suggests is sufficient.

Scent-Verified Posting

Every upload is authenticated against a marked perimeter before it enters the relay. If the scent does not match the geotag, the post is held for review by an unaffiliated pack with no standing in the dispute. Appeals resolve within one season.
