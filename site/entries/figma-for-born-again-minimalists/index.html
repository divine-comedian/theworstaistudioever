Governance

Subtraction Review

Every object you keep needs written justification from a colleague, filed within 24 hours. Approvals lapse after 30 days and the object is removed on the 31st. Nobody is notified, because notifying you would be a form of clutter.

PA.2026.0011 · Review workflow, mandatory, two signatories.

Capacity

The Eleven

A Pare file holds eleven objects. Placing a twelfth opens a dialog asking which of the eleven you love less. No answer keeps twelve. Teams describe the first week as difficult and the second week as quiet.
